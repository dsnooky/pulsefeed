Editorial policy

Third-party feeds

Third-party RSS items are presented as headlines, limited excerpts where supplied, source names and links to the original publisher. Their reporting remains the publisher's work. We respect feed terms and remove or adjust material when a rights holder raises a valid concern.

Original articles

PulseFeed-labelled articles should be researched and written by our editorial team. Authors must verify facts, identify sources, distinguish fact from opinion, and avoid plagiarism.

AI assistance

AI may assist with summaries or editing, but it is not a source. Editors must verify claims against reliable primary sources before publication. AI-generated text must not be presented as independently verified reporting.

Corrections

Readers and publishers may contact the editorial team to report errors, attribution issues or removal requests. We will review requests promptly and correct material when warranted.
